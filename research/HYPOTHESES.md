# Research Hypotheses

## Research question

How do abnormal increases in network traffic affect server response time, and can multiple and polynomial regression models explain and predict this effect?

## Hypotheses

- **H1:** Packet rate has a significant positive effect on response time when the other variables are held constant.
- **H2:** The regression model explains a significant part of the variation in response time, as assessed by the overall F-test.
- **H3:** Adding polynomial terms improves the model significantly over the linear model.
- **H4 (supporting):** Mean response time differs across traffic levels, as assessed by one-way ANOVA.

These hypotheses concern the simulated server system. The research question, variables, and models are defined in the project README and research methodology.
